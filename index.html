Mission

Why this open source space exists

Building konnek, we keep creating things that are useful beyond our own product: connectors, small tools, and skills for working with AI agents. We would rather share them openly than keep them in a drawer.

This is where they live. Everything here is meant to help you connect with konnek – or simply to solve a problem you happen to share with us.

What we stand for

Simple, European, independent

  • SimpleSmall, clear solutions that “just run” – no integration project needed.
  • EuropeanData stays in Germany, and we actively connect European software alternatives.
  • AI-nativeDescribe an integration in plain language and let konnek build it.
  • IndependentBootstrapped, without investor money, and growing towards shared ownership.
