konnek

Connecting the tools your business already uses.

konnek makes integrations simple for small and medium-sized businesses in Europe: set up in a few steps, hosted GDPR-compliant in Germany, with AI built in from day one.

Why this open source space exists

Building konnek, we keep creating things that are useful beyond our own product: connectors, small tools, and skills for working with AI agents. We want to share them openly instead of keeping them in a drawer.

This is where they live. Everything here is meant to help you connect with konnek – or simply to solve a problem you happen to share with us.

What you will find here

Integrations

Connectors and examples for linking your software – especially European tools – with konnek.

Capabilities

Building blocks and interfaces that let other systems talk to konnek.

Tools

Developer utilities we built for ourselves and found worth sharing.

Skills

Reusable instructions and workflows for AI coding agents, refined during our own work.

What we stand for

  • Simple. Small, clear solutions that “just run” – no integration project needed.
  • European. Data stays in Germany, and we actively connect European software alternatives.
  • AI-native. Describe an integration in plain language and let konnek build it.
  • Independent. Bootstrapped, without investor money, and growing towards shared ownership.
